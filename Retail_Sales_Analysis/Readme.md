# 📈 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built to monitor sales performance, revenue growth, customer behavior, and profitability. The dashboard helps stakeholders make informed business decisions through real-time visual insights.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to monitor sales performance across multiple regions, products, and customer segments. This dashboard centralizes sales data to provide actionable insights for improving business performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total sales and revenue
* Track profit margins
* Monitor monthly sales trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Compare regional performance
* Improve business decision-making

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Sales Transactions
* Customer Information
* Product Details
* Regional Sales
* Order Information
