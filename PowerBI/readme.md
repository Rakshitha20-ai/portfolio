# 🌍 Global Climate & Energy Analysis Dashboard (2020–2024)

## 📌 Overview

The **Global Climate & Energy Dashboard (2020–2024)** is an interactive Power BI project that analyzes climate indicators and global energy consumption trends across multiple countries. The dashboard provides actionable insights into renewable energy adoption, carbon emissions, temperature changes, and energy production to support data-driven environmental and policy decisions.

---

# ❓ Problem Statement

Governments, environmental organizations, and energy companies require accurate insights into climate change and energy consumption to develop sustainable strategies.

This dashboard consolidates climate and energy datasets into a single interactive reporting solution, enabling users to monitor trends, compare countries, and identify opportunities for cleaner energy adoption.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze global climate trends from 2020–2024.
* Monitor renewable and non-renewable energy consumption.
* Compare CO₂ emissions across countries.
* Track temperature and climate indicators.
* Evaluate energy production by source.
* Support sustainable decision-making using data visualization.

---

# 📂 Dataset

The dashboard uses global climate and energy datasets containing:

* Country

* Average Temperature
* CO₂ Emissions
* Renewable Energy Production
* Fossil Fuel Consumption
* Solar Energy
* Wind Energy
* Hydropower
* Electricity Generation
* Energy Consumption
* Population (if applicable)
